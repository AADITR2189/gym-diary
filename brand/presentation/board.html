What we heard

The brief

A private, offline gym and cardio diary for one person. It should feel consistent, personal and quietly athletic: the opposite of loud fitness apps full of flames and dumbbells.

consistentpersonaldisciplinedathletic

Success: no fitness clichés, recognisable as an app icon and at 16 px, works in one colour, matches the app's navy/yellow and teal/sand palette.

Steps

A slice of your training calendar: the days you logged climb like steps. Showing up is progress.

  • Built on the app's own yearly training grid, so logo and app feel like one thing
  • Avoids every gym cliché (no dumbbell, flame or flexing arm)
  • A 3×3 grid of one radius and one gap: crisp as a phone icon and still a staircase at 16 px
